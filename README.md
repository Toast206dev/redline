## ✨ What it does

Redline is an overlay menu that runs on top of the game (Azimuth). Press **Insert** to open it. If you have no Insert key, pick any key, controller or wheel button the first time you play.

### 🏎️ Driving
- **Boost**, **nitrous** with pops & bangs, anti-lag and burnouts
- **Launch control**: standing, rolling and wheelie launches, plus **rolling hold** at any speed
- **Handling**: adjustable drift, extra grip, super brakes, speed limiter, **traction control & ABS**
- **Stunts**: barrel roll, 360 spin, backflip, rocket boost, jump, wheelie mode
- **Air control**, low gravity and **UFO mode** (fly the car)
- **Ghost mode** (drive through traffic) and **teleport** to saved spots

### ⏱️ Tools
- Run timers from **0-60 to 0-200 MPH** and **1/8 mile to 1 mile**, with best times and **run history**
- **Drag mode** with a light tree, reaction times and **1v1 drag races**
- **Telemetry graph & dyno**: power and torque curves from one pull
- Real on-screen **gauges** (speedo, boost, nitrous) and **HUD layouts**, movable and resizable
- **Presets** with a **preset browser** to find and share setups
- **Tools & search** (Ctrl+F), "What does this do?" mode, controller navigation, hover help on every setting

### 🛠️ Game
- **Build your own parts**: turbos, superchargers, cams, tune, tyres, weight, ride height, engine swaps
- **Stance & wheels**: front/rear-only rim sizes and tyre widths, tyre sidewall, axle width
- Ready-made **packs**, unlimited tuning sliders, free cars (live)
- **Your saves**: level, wristband, XP and credits for every save. Add **credits, XP, wheelspins and skill points**, or copy progress between saves (always backed up first)

### 🌐 Online *(ForzaInfinity · Infinity Horizon · FH2MP)*
- **Lobby**: players in your world with distance, speed and car; see who else runs Redline
- **Global chat**, lobby chat, private messages, **profiles, friends and reactions**
- **Global leaderboard** with weekly challenges, **events**, car of the week and badges

### 🎨 Look
- Any menu colour, many backgrounds, menu size 80-130%
- 🌍 **Español · Français · Deutsch · Português · Italiano**

## 📦 Install
1. Download **Redline.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Toast206dev/redline/releases/latest).
2. Right-click the zip → **Properties** → tick **Unblock** → OK, then extract it.
3. Run the game in **borderless or windowed** mode, open **Redline.exe**, and drive in free roam.

Already on 1.7.5 or newer? Just click **Update now** in Redline.

> Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (click **More info → Run anyway**), and some antivirus tools flag programs that change a running game. That's what Redline does.

## 🔒 Good to know
- Driving effects only change **your own car**, and switch off when Redline closes.
- Every change to game files or your save is **backed up first**.
- The online features use Toast's own service. **READ ME FIRST.txt** (in the zip) explains exactly what is sent.
